- **URL inspector (§6.2):** registrable domain via tldts; `isOfficial` against the claimed brand when the message names one, otherwise against any known owner; lookalikes by (a) brand token in the label, (b) homoglyph map (Cyrillic, Greek, 0/1/5/@/$, `rn→m`, `vv→w`, `cl→d`) + Damerau-Levenshtein, including the official label on another suffix (`icicibank.xyz`), (c) brand token in a subdomain (`sbi.secure-login.top`); `lookalikeOf` and a character-level `lookalikeDiff` (indexes into `host`); punycode decoded for display (`host` is Unicode); shortener, raw IP, risky TLD, punycode, APK, plain-http login path; risk = noisy-OR, official links capped at 10.
- **Network extras:** `inspectUrlNetwork` / `inspectUrlsNetwork` call `/api/expand` (shorteners and non-official links; the final URL is inspected once, REDIRECT_CHAIN on ≥ 3 hops or a cross-domain risky final URL) and `/api/domain-age` (non-official, non-IP; NEW_DOMAIN < 90 days, weight 0.45 under 30 days) in parallel, only through `net.ts`, with a hard 3-second total budget (a `fetch` that ignores its AbortSignal still cannot hold the scan), silent fallback offline or on errors. `analyze()` runs them when `options.expandLinks` / `options.domainAge` allow (default on) and sets `offline: false` only when a call returned data. Tested against mocked `fetch`.
- **UPI (§6.3):** PSP handle table, conservative `looksPersonal`, UPI_NAME_MISMATCH + PERSONAL_UPI_HANDLE when a personal UPI ID is presented as a brand or institution (brand token in the local part, brand named within 30 chars before the ID, or a QR payee / collect requester name that claims a brand or "refund desk"-style institution). - **QR (§6.4):** `upi://pay` → payee checks, PREFILLED_AMOUNT, QR_TO_RECEIVE for receive/refund/cashback/prize wording in `pn`/`tn` in four languages (floor 85); `upi://collect` → COLLECT_REQUEST; a URL → "This QR opens a website, not a payment" plus the URL inspector; other text → analysed as a message. - **PSP handles not verified against NPCI:** NPCI's member and third-party-app pages render client-side and could not be read; only Paytm and Amazon Pay handles were confirmed from secondary reports (comment in `upi/handles.ts`).
- **Deviations from SPEC_A, each documented in code:**
  - Single-item inputs (bare URL, UPI ID or phone, or a QR holding only a URL) leave the rules layer out of the §8.2 average — there is no message text for it to judge; otherwise a non-bank lookalike link scored ~19 ("No warning signs found").
  - Lookalike distance matching applies to tokens of ≥ 5 letters (distance 1 for 5, 2 for longer); tokens of ≤ 4 letters (sbi, pnb, hdfc, trai) match only exactly — distance 1 on them matched ordinary words ("train" ≈ "trai"). Typos must also keep the first letter and roughly the length.
  - BRAND_MISMATCH, UPI_NAME_MISMATCH and PERSONAL_UPI_HANDLE are rules-layer signals (identity evidence from the message), not links-layer.
  - A QR that opens a non-official website gets a floor of 40 (suspicious) — SPEC.md M4.2 lists it as a flag; on its own it would score ≈ 10.
  - Phone-based BRAND_MISMATCH (official claim + personal mobile) needs a threat or pressure signal, so a calm genuine outage notice with a lineman's number is not flagged.
  - `ASKS_OTP` floor 45 (from P1) kept.
- `claimedAs` stays keyword-based ("electricity officer" → `police`).
- Brand words like "jio", "challan", "aadhaar" can create claims in messages that only mention them; they only matter together with a mismatching link, a personal UPI ID, or a personal mobile number under pressure.
- Carried: tool escaping quirks on this laptop (`\uXXXX` and `\\` in tool input), root scripts use `--workspaces --if-present`, TypeScript `~6.0.3`, Vercel compiling `api/*.ts` with `.ts` workspace imports unverified, newline collapse in chat/email text, `OcrResult.confidence` 0–100, `eval-report.json` placeholders.
